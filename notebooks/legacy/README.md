# Controlled benchmark archive

`P2_01_controlled_evaluation.ipynb` and `.py` preserve the previous controlled
BGE/Qwen experiments over `data/test/reranker_eval` and Kaggle-generated cases.
They do not consume the P1-11 handoff; labels are heuristically generated by
`scripts/build_reranker_testcases.py`, not reviewed relevance judgments.

Known limitations are retained, not silently fixed: Recall@10 on at most seven
IR candidates is insensitive to rank; cross-lingual delta is top-1 sigmoid-score
difference, not delta NDCG/MRR. Contraindication/population labels need review.
These notebooks also retain their old setup paths and output behavior.

Use `../P2_01_reranker_evaluation.ipynb` or `scripts/benchmark_reranking.py` for the
P1-11 candidate/label benchmark. No notebook or previous experiment logic was
deleted during the migration.
